# Typekeeper 3.2.0 — pacing, pressure score and feedback

Basis: the user-supplied v3.1 ZIP and the user's actual playtest report that the
opening ramps too slowly. No claim of measured original-video parity.

## Earlier challenge
Classic now starts at 36 rather than 28 logical pixels/second; chapter 6 reaches
58 rather than 34.4. Ordinary spawn intervals move from 1.90 to 0.96 seconds across
48 chapters. Medium words begin entering chapter 2. The dictionary, quotas, trials,
spells, scoring formula and mastery requirements do not change. Full numerical
curves and the retained no-waste/late-submit/retry safeguards are in RULES.md.

## Pressure and recovery
Two newly authored, phase-aligned music layers add ostinatos, pulse and faster
articulation as pile pressure rises. A second urgency layer enters above 65%.
WIND quickly releases both without restarting or pitching the original music.
Pause/results settle the mix. An independent switch restores the baseline score.
All previous art and original musical masters/runtime stems are preserved.

## Word, item and score feel
Mechanical key variants; tiny matching-letter glints; a full-word-ready chime;
completion paper sheen and score sparks; separate book, dark-card and streak cues;
weighted misses, contained errors, four distinct spell treatments, page turns,
and one subtle cue for each natural ICE/SLOW ending. Queued SLOW still waits
behind ICE. Large streak text gives way to live word cards. Unchanged HUD text/markup is no
longer rebuilt on every tick, preserving nodes and reducing avoidable rendering work.

Chapter bonuses count upward briefly with bounded ticks, then seal the awarded
stars. Points are already saved. Click the total to skip, or use Next immediately.
Leaving the screen cancels old tally voices; revisiting it does not replay the count.
Reduced motion and mute remain effective; shimmer has its own switch.

## Migration and delivery
New key/ruleset 3.2. Earlier valid settings, unlocks, stars and bookmarks migrate
without changing old storage. Previous totals and continued mixed-rule runs remain
Legacy. A fresh game earns current records. Includes a tested backup-first updater
for the existing local Git/Vercel project; it does not publish automatically.

The automated results and limits are in QA_REPORT.md. Human difficulty/fun and
music listening, physical device/browser checks and native Steam delivery remain open.
