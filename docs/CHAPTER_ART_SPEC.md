## Boundaries

All prominent scene decoration stays in the shelf areas. Room surfaces are cached with a two-entry cap. Small environmental motion uses the existing slowed/frozen world clock and is removed with Ambient animation OFF. ICE state remains readable without movement. Endless reuses the final profile instead of creating unbounded artwork.

The two new render modules are authored Canvas art, not photos or frames extracted from a reference video.
