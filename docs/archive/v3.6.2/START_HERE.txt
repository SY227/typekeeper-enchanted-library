A Fresh Journey

Extract the ENTIRE ZIP. Open PLAY.html for the self-contained offline game.
Mac local server: bash START_MAC.command
The launcher and title should show v3.6.2.

Returning players have three clear choices:
  Continue: resume your saved campaign chapter.
  Start from Chapter 1: confirm a fresh attempt, keeping all lifetime progress.
  Chapters: practice any unlocked chapter without replacing campaign Continue.

Starting again replaces ONLY the selected difficulty's campaign Continue point.
Unlocked chapters, mastery stars, recorded best scores and other difficulties stay.
Escape / Close / Keep current journey cancels without changing the save.

Before changing browser, URL, port or local-file location: Records -> Export save.
Stop an old local server with Control+C to reuse its address; import when needed.
